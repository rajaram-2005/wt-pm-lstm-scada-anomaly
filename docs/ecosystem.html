How the 25 models relate

One record's journey through the collection:

  1. Sense & compress (Edge AI). AeroZip (2) squeezes high-dimensional telemetry into a latent stream for cellular/satellite backhaul; the SNN (19) and quantized MobileNet (17) turn raw high-frequency vibration into events and features on-device; TinyML relays (22) run distilled trees from the classifiers for microsecond safety trips.
  2. Detect (SCADA anomaly intelligence). Seven detectors watch the same 12-channel contract from different angles — LSTM sequences (13), GRU streams (9), VAE reconstruction (23), Deep SVDD boundaries (6), Isolation Forest outliers (12), TCN power-curve tracking (21) and Informer long-horizon forecasts (11). All of them emit the same wt-pm.anomaly.v1 record.
  3. Classify (fault intelligence). Anomaly records are triaged into named faults: XGBoost (25) and Random Forest (18) on tabular features, the 1D-CNN (1) and SVM-RBF (20) on component signatures, contrastive SSL (3) supplying labels-free features, and the GNN (8) lifting per-turbine records to fleet-level cascade risk.
  4. Project (RUL prognostics). Classified degradation drives remaining-useful-life: HMM states (10) seed the particle filter (15), ConvLSTM (4) and DBN features (5) track wear, and the MLP regressor (14) provides the honest baseline every fancier model must beat.
  5. Constrain & explain (physics + XAI). The digital-twin surrogate (7) supplies physics residuals back to the detectors, the PG-BNN (16) injects thermodynamic constraints and calibrated uncertainty into prognostics, and SHAP/LIME (24) turns every black-box alert into a root-cause explanation a maintenance engineer can act on.
