Advanced concepts

The ideas this model actually leans on, each stated with its failure mode.

Baseline discipline

A prognostic result without a baseline is unfalsifiable. Because this model is in the same registry, same protocol and same held-out split, every claim from ConvLSTM, HMM or the particle filter can be stated as an improvement over a number that is visible.

Proxy targets

RUL is not measured in the simulator; time-to-fault-onset is. The target is a proxy, and the fidelity ladder says so - which is why 'hours' on these pages are comparable across models and not to a real asset.

Where an MLP wins

On short records with strong monotone covariates, a small MLP is hard to beat and impossible to beat on cost. Its weakness is exactly that it cannot represent sequence state - the gap the recurrent and filtering models are supposed to close.
