Advanced concepts

The ideas this model actually leans on, each stated with its failure mode.

Why a surrogate at all

FEA of a blade or tower is accurate and unusable online. A surrogate trained offline reproduces the input-output map in milliseconds, which is what makes physics available inside a 10-minute SCADA loop.

Residuals as a detector

The model never claims to find faults. It predicts what physics implies, and the mismatch between prediction and measurement is handed to the anomaly voters - physics constrains the learning rather than replacing it.

Extrapolation is the failure mode

A surrogate is only valid inside its training envelope. Outside rated wind or in a new control strategy its residuals grow for reasons that have nothing to do with damage, which is why residuals are clustered with, never substituted for, the statistical detectors.
