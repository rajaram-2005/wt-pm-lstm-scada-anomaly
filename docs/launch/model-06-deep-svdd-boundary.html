Deep SVDD Boundary

A neural network trained to map healthy telemetry into a tight hypersphere, so that abnormality is literally distance from the centre. Unlike reconstruction models it never learns what a fault looks like - it only learns the shape of normal, which is exactly the assumption a wind farm can support.

Advanced concepts

The ideas this model actually leans on, each stated with its failure mode.

Hypersphere collapse

If you let a network map everything to one point, the loss goes to zero and the model dies. The classical fixes are the bias-free encoder plus weight decay - both are present here, and both are the reason this is a usable detector rather than a degenerate one.

One-class training

Deep SVDD trains on healthy data only. That is its strength (no fault labels needed) and its blind spot: it cannot distinguish a new normal operating regime from a fault unless the encoders around it disagree.

Centre matters more than weights

init_center() from a healthy sample biases the whole solution. Averaging over a representative healthy window - and refusing to move c afterwards - is what makes the anomaly score comparable across turbines.
