DBN Feature Extraction

A stack of Restricted Boltzmann Machines trained greedily layer by layer, then read out as a feature hierarchy for wear tracking. It is the classic pre-deep-learning answer to 'we do not have enough labels', and it still earns its place because its features are interpretable as successive energy minima.

Advanced concepts

The ideas this model actually leans on, each stated with its failure mode.

Energy-based models

An RBM defines an energy for every visible-hidden configuration; learning lowers the energy of data and raises it elsewhere. The gradient needs the model's own expectation, which is intractable - that is the whole reason for contrastive divergence.

Why one Gibbs step is enough

CD-1 is biased but cheap: it does not follow the true gradient, yet it reliably moves parameters in a useful direction. The bias is why DBNs were displaced by backpropagation-based autoencoders, and why the repo's CD-1 loop matters for reproducing the numbers.

Layerwise features as a ladder

Each layer's activations are a legitimate feature set, so you can stop early for a cheaper model. The platform treats the DBN as a feature provider, which is how it can serve an RUL regressor without retraining anything downstream.
