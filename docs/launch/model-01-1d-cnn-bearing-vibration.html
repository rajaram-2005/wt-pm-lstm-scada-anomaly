1D-CNN Bearing Vibration

A three-block 1D convolutional network that classifies gearbox bearing condition directly from a raw vibration window, with no hand-built spectral features. It is the highest-resolution view of the drivetrain in the collection: the only model that would consume a real high-frequency DAQ stream unchanged if one were wired to the simulator.

Advanced concepts

The ideas this model actually leans on, each stated with its failure mode.

Receptive field arithmetic

Three kernel-3 convolutions separated by max-pool 2 give a receptive field of 1 + 2 + 4 + ... samples of context per output activation. Widening the kernel or adding a dilation is the only way to see a full shaft revolution without a deeper stack - the reason the upstream model stops at 1024 samples.

Convolution beats handcrafted spectra

Classical bearing diagnostics hand you envelope spectra and defect frequencies (BPFO/BPFI). A 1D-CNN learns its own filter bank end to end, which is why it transfers across machines - and why it needs thousands of labelled windows to beat the handcrafted baseline at all.

Surrogate-data honesty

The platform has 10-minute RMS SCADA, not 20 kHz DAQ. Waveforms are synthesised and carry vibration_is_surrogate=True, so every metric this model reports is a property of the pipeline, not of a real bearing. Swapping in a real DAQ path changes only the adapter's input view, never the network.
