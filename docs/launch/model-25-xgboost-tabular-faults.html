Advanced concepts

The ideas this model actually leans on, each stated with its failure mode.

The second-order step

Boosting with gradient and curvature information converges in far fewer rounds than first-order boosting on the same data. The price is a tighter coupling between loss and implementation - which is why the fallback to m10 exists.

Regularisation is what prevents memorising the plant

Leaf-weight penalties and tree complexity terms are the main reason a boosted ensemble does not simply memorise turbine-specific quirks. They are also why the model can be retrained per site without collapsing.

Explainability by construction

TreeExplainer computes exact Shapley values for this model in polynomial time, so the strongest classifier also produces the most rigorous explanation - the platform's XAI story rests on that pairing.

Distillation target

Its behaviour is what the m25 edge relay approximates with a depth-5 tree, and what m24 compresses to INT8. Accuracy at the centre buys simplicity at the edge.

Honest limits
